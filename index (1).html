Questions parents and students ask

Who can join?

Children and adults, beginners and those who already read. We place each student at the right level.

Can we try a class first?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp to arrange a free trial class and meet your teacher before you decide.

What do I need for the class?

A phone, tablet or computer with internet, and the free Zoom app. A Quran and a quiet place to sit are enough.

Can I join from another country?

Yes. Our students join from around the world, and class timings are arranged to suit your time zone.

How do I register?

Fill in the admission form or message us on WhatsApp at 0309-5428928. We will reply with the next steps.
