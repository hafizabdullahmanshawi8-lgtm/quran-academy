What you can learn

Start from the alphabet or build on what you already know. We place each student at the right level.

Basic Qaida

Learn to read the Quran from the very beginning.

Quran Reading (Nazra)

Read the Quran and improve your recitation.

Tajweed

Learn the rules of Tajweed and apply them in your recitation.

Hifz

Memorize the Quran with steady, personal guidance.

Tafseer of the Quran

Fluency in Talawat

Basic Islamic Studies

Personal attention, in a live class

Every class is one-to-one, so the teacher can listen to your recitation, correct it on the spot and move at your pace. Classes run online, so there is no travel and you choose a time that fits school, work and family life.

Our teachers are experienced in teaching the Quran to children and adults. Message us to arrange a trial class and meet your teacher first.
